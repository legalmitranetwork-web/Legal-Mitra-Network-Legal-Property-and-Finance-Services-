Our Genesis

Bridging Complexity with Trust & Technology

Legal Mitra Network was founded to solve a persistent challenge in India: fragmented services across property verification, bank loan approvals, and legal counsel. Traditionally, citizens had to consult separate lawyers, CA firms, property brokers, and bank DSA agents, leading to delays and lack of transparency.

We unified these domain pillars onto a single digital ecosystem. By pairing top-tier advocates and financial experts with a Turtlemint-style POSP partner model, we enable independent advisors and brokers to offer comprehensive financial and legal guidance to clients in every corner of India.

• Established corporate headquarters in Mumbai with 15 senior Advocates and CAs.

• Digitized property title search reports across 10 major urban hubs in Maharashtra and Karnataka.

• Integrated multi-bank loan comparison engine for 40+ lending institutions.

• Launched the Agent Partner Portal allowing POSP advisors to track referral payouts live.

• Over 25,000+ active referral partner agents nationwide.

• Processed ₹250 Cr+ in loan disbursements and completed 10,000+ legal title document verifications.
