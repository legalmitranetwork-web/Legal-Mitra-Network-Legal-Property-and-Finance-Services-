Refund & Cancellation Policy

Clear guidelines regarding service execution, professional fees, statutory payments, and partner commission adjustments.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Last Updated: March 2026

1. Overview & Scope

At Legal Mitra Network, we maintain transparency across our property title search, mortgage syndication, legal drafting, and POSP affiliate network. This Refund & Cancellation Policy outlines the conditions under which cancellations are accepted and professional fees or service retainers are refunded.

2. Cancellation Before Work Begins

If you cancel a requested service or legal drafting engagement before our team or assigned panel expert has initiated preparation or government filing work, you are entitled to a refund of your professional fee, subject to a nominal payment gateway processing deduction.

3. Statutory & Government Fees Non-Refundability

Many of our integrated services (such as property registration levies, stamp duties, company registrations, or statutory filing charges) involve government or regulatory fees collected on your behalf:

  • Once statutory fees have been dispatched or deposited with government portals or authorities, they are strictly non-refundable under any circumstances.
  • Government authorities do not issue refunds for submitted applications once processing has commenced, regardless of final outcome or client withdrawal.

4. Refund Eligibility After Work Has Commenced

Once our panel advocates, financial consultants, or technical experts have started executing your task, refunds are administered based on completion phases:

  • Full Refund of Professional Fee: Applicable only if Legal Mitra Network fails to deliver the agreed service due to internal default, or in cases of proven duplicate transactions.
  • Partial Refund: If you cancel after preliminary drafting or title search reviews have begun but prior to final document execution, the professional fee will be refunded minus the standard value of work already performed.
  • No Refund: Once complete legal reports, executed sale deeds, or loan files are submitted to banks or statutory bodies, professional fees are non-refundable.

Note for POSP & Channel Partners: Affiliate commissions or network override bonuses credited against cancelled or fraudulent client transactions will be dynamically adjusted or debited from the partner's digital wallet balance.

5. Request Timeline & Processing Window

To request a review of your case regarding cancellations or refunds:

  • Raise your ticket within 30 days of making the payment or encountering the service dispute.
  • Submit requests via email to support@legalmitranetwork.com detailing your transaction reference and case ID.
  • Verified refunds are reviewed within 5–7 business days and processed back to the original payment source within 7–14 business days.
