Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about real estate legal title checks and registrations.

Why is a 30-Year Title Search Report mandatory before buying property?

A 30-year search report ensures that the seller has absolute legal ownership and that the property is free from undisclosed bank mortgages, legal suits, family partition disputes, or government acquisition claims. Banks also mandate this for home loan approvals.

How long does online Leave & License Rent Agreement registration take?

Once doorstep biometric verification is completed for both Licensor and Licensee, the document is submitted to the government e-registration portal. Official registered copies with QR codes are generated typically within 24 to 48 working hours.

Can POSP Agents and Real Estate Brokers submit property leads for clients?

Yes! Licensed POSP partners and channel brokers can submit title search or agreement drafting leads directly through the Agent Portal and track live status and earn referral commissions upon successful service completion.

What is the difference between 7/12 Extract and Property Card (CTS)?

A 7/12 Extract is a revenue record for rural or semi-urban land indicating ownership and crop details maintained by the Talathi. A Property Card (CTS Number) is the urban land equivalent issued by City Survey offices for properties situated within municipal limits.
