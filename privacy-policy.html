01

Introduction & Scope

Legal Mitra Network ("we", "our", or "us"), operating as a comprehensive pan-India enablement platform for Property, Finance, and Legal Solutions along with a Point of Sales Person (POSP) partner network, is deeply committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of our users, clients, and affiliate partners.

This Privacy Policy governs your use of our website (legalmitranetwork.com), mobile applications, agent dashboards, and integrated financial calculators. By accessing our platform or registering as a client or POSP partner, you explicitly consent to the collection, storage, use, and disclosure of your information as described herein.

02

Information We Collect

To provide seamless property title scrutiny, home loan processing, and POSP commission payouts, we collect various categories of information:

Personal Identifiable Data

Full name, mobile number, email address, residential address, date of birth, and professional credentials for registered agents.

Financial & Loan Data

PAN card, Aadhaar number, CIBIL score reports, bank account statements, salary slips, and income tax returns (ITR).

03

KYC & Document Verification

In compliance with Reserve Bank of India (RBI), National Housing Bank (NHB), and insurance regulatory guidelines for home loans and POSP onboarding, we conduct rigorous Know Your Customer (KYC) verifications.

  • Digital Aadhaar and PAN validation through secure government-approved API gateways.
  • Property title deed examination, Index-II search extracts, and encumbrance certificate storage.
  • Biometric or OTP-based consent authentication prior to document submission to partner banking institutions.
04

Affiliate & POSP Tracking

Legal Mitra Network utilizes a Turtlesmint Pro-style partner ecosystem. When you interact with our platform via a registered POSP agent or affiliate marketing link:

Cookie & Referral Link Attribution

We store secure tracking cookies and referral tokens to attribute loan applications, insurance leads, and legal advisory consultations accurately to your recommending agent for correct commission calculation.

05

Data Security & Storage

We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical security safeguards conforming to ISO 27001 standards to protect your sensitive personal and financial data.

Encryption in Transit

All data transferred is encrypted via 256-bit SSL protocols.

Secure Cloud Storage

Hosted on secure Indian data center servers with strict role-based access.

Access Auditing

Regular automated security audits and access log monitoring.

06

Sharing & Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party marketing agencies. However, information may be shared under strict confidentiality agreements with:

  • Scheduled commercial banks and housing finance companies (HFCs) for home loan underwriting.
  • Panel advocates, chartered accountants, and legal experts assisting in title due diligence.
  • Law enforcement agencies or regulatory authorities when mandated by Indian statutory law.
07

User Rights & Choices

As a registered user or POSP partner on Legal Mitra Network, you retain full ownership of your data rights under applicable data protection legislation:

Right to Access & Rectify

You can review, edit, or update your profile and KYC details at any time directly through your dashboard.

Right to Data Erasure

You may request deletion of your account and non-regulatory data records by submitting a formal privacy request.
