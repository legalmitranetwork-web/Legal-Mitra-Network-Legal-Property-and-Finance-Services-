Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding response timelines, document uploads, and consultation security.

How fast will an advocate or loan specialist call me back?
Our standard SLA guarantees a callback within 2 business hours for inquiries submitted between 9 AM and 6 PM IST. Urgent legal notice requests are routed directly to our priority queue.
Is my initial consultation confidential?
Yes. All communications and property documents shared with Legal Mitra Network are governed by advocate-client privilege and strict non-disclosure policies.
Can I upload my property documents directly through the portal?
Yes, once you submit your initial inquiry, you will receive an automated link to your secure Client Portal where you can upload land titles, bank statements, and ID proofs safely.
